Impressions alone beats the composite.

Isolating each feature on the same test split, impressions alone reaches 8.90× lift — edging out the combined within-client score at 8.76×. Word count in isolation barely clears the 1× floor a random ranking would produce.

The honest reading: this score is functionally an impressions-based ranking. Word count's inclusion is reported as untested, not beneficial — exactly the kind of finding a naive summary would have buried.
